# Garment care

## Cotton

Machine wash at 30 or 40 degrees with similar colours. Cotton t-shirts can be tumble dried on low, but drying flat keeps their shape longer. Expect up to 3% shrinkage on the first wash.

## Linen

Machine wash at 30 degrees on a gentle cycle and do not tumble dry. Hang to dry and iron while slightly damp. Linen creases naturally and becomes softer with every wash.

## Wool and merino

Hand wash in cool water with wool detergent, or use the wool programme at no more than 30 degrees. Never tumble dry or wring. Reshape and dry flat on a towel away from direct heat. Small bobbles (pilling) in the first weeks are normal and can be removed with a fabric comb.

## Denim

Wash inside out at 30 degrees, and wash as rarely as you can to keep the colour. Dark denim can transfer colour onto light fabrics and bags during the first few wears. Do not tumble dry stretch jeans, because heat damages the elastane.

## Outerwear and waterproofs

Close all zips and wash at 30 degrees without fabric softener, which blocks the water-repellent finish. Puffer jackets can be tumble dried on low with two clean tennis balls to restore the padding. Rain coats should be wiped clean and hung to dry.

## Viscose

Hand wash cold or use a delicate cycle at 30 degrees. Viscose is weaker when wet, so do not wring it. Hang to dry and steam rather than iron.
