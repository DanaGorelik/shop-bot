# Store information

## About Olive Lane

Olive Lane is an independent clothing shop selling everyday basics, knitwear, linen pieces and outerwear for adults. We have one physical shop and an online store. All prices are in Israeli shekels (₪) and include VAT.

## Address and opening hours

The shop is at 12 HaTavor Street, Tel Aviv, on the ground floor next to the bakery.

Opening hours:
- Sunday to Thursday: 09:30 to 20:00
- Friday and holiday eves: 09:00 to 14:30
- Saturday: closed

The shop is closed on public holidays. Online orders can be placed at any time.

## Contacting us

The fastest way to reach us is this WhatsApp chat. You can also email hello@olivelane.example or call the shop on 03-555-0199 during opening hours. A member of the team replies to messages that need a person within one business day.

## Payment methods

Online we accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Bit and PayPal. In the shop we also accept cash. Orders above ₪400 can be split into up to three interest-free card payments. We do not accept cheques.
