# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery options and prices

- Standard home delivery: ₪25, arrives in 3 to 5 business days.
- Express home delivery: ₪45, arrives in 1 to 2 business days. Express is available for orders placed before 13:00, Sunday to Thursday.
- Pickup from the shop: free. Your order is ready within 24 hours and we send a message when you can collect it. We hold pickup orders for 10 days.

## Free shipping

Standard delivery is free on orders of ₪300 or more after discounts. Express delivery is never free, but on orders of ₪300 or more it costs ₪20 instead of ₪45.

## Where we deliver

We deliver everywhere in Israel. We do not ship abroad at the moment. Deliveries to Eilat and the Arava can take up to 7 business days.

## Tracking an order

When your order leaves our warehouse you receive an SMS from the courier, Swift Parcel, with a tracking number and a link. Orders with the status "processing" have not left the warehouse yet and do not have a tracking number.

## Late or missing deliveries

If a standard delivery has not arrived 7 business days after it was shipped, tell us and we will open an investigation with the courier. If the parcel is lost we send a replacement or refund you in full, whichever you prefer.

## Changing or cancelling an order

You can change the address, the items or cancel an order free of charge as long as its status is "processing". Once an order is "shipped" it can no longer be changed; you can refuse the delivery or return it under the returns policy.
