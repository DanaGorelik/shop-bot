# Frequently asked questions

## Gift cards

Digital gift cards are available from ₪100 to ₪1,000 and are sent by email within a few minutes. They are valid for 5 years, can be used online and in the shop, and can be used across several purchases. Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Gift wrapping

We gift wrap any order for ₪10 per parcel and can add a handwritten note for free. Gift orders are sent without prices on the packing slip. The person receiving a gift can exchange it within the normal 30 days using the gift receipt.

## Olive Lane Club

Joining the club is free. Members earn 1 point for every ₪1 spent, and every 500 points become a ₪25 voucher. Members also get 10% off during their birthday month and early access to sales. Points expire after 12 months without a purchase.

## Discounts for students and soldiers

Students and soldiers get 10% off full-price items in the shop when they show a valid card. This discount is not available online and cannot be combined with other offers.

## Alterations

We hem jeans and trousers for free in the shop, usually while you wait. Other alterations, such as taking in a waist or shortening sleeves, cost ₪40 to ₪90 and take about 5 business days. Altered items cannot be returned.

## Restocks and out-of-stock items

Core items such as t-shirts, jeans and merino sweaters are restocked every two to three weeks. Seasonal colours are usually not restocked once they sell out. We cannot reserve items that are out of stock, but the shop team can tell you if a delivery is expected.

## Reserving an item in the shop

You can ask in this chat to hold an in-stock item at the shop for 48 hours, free of charge. Each hold is for one item in one colour and size, and you can have up to two holds at a time. You pay when you collect it. Holds that are not collected within 48 hours end automatically, and you can cancel a hold in this chat at any time.

## Sustainability

Our cotton is certified organic and our padded jackets use recycled polyester. Parcels are sent in recycled paper mailers without plastic.
