# Returns and exchanges

## Return window

You can return an item within 30 days of the day it was delivered or collected. After 30 days we cannot accept a return unless the item is faulty.

## Condition of returned items

Items must be unworn, unwashed and have their original tags attached. We may refuse a return, or offer store credit only, if an item shows signs of wear.

## Items that cannot be returned

For hygiene reasons we cannot take back sleepwear, including pyjama sets, once the packaging has been opened. Gift cards cannot be returned. Items that were altered or hemmed for you cannot be returned.

## Sale items

Items bought at a discount of 30% or more can be exchanged or returned for store credit, but not refunded to your payment method.

## Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 business days of us receiving the item. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. The original delivery fee is not refunded unless the item was faulty or we sent the wrong item.

## Exchanges

Exchanging for a different size or colour of the same item is free the first time, including delivery of the new item. A second exchange on the same order costs ₪20. If the new item costs more you pay the difference; if it costs less we refund the difference.

## How to return an item

1. Tell us your order number and which item you want to return, here on WhatsApp.
2. We send you a return label by message.
3. Hand the parcel to any Swift Parcel collection point, or bring it to the shop.

Returning by courier costs ₪20, which we deduct from the refund. Returning in the shop is free.

## Faulty or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged, has a manufacturing fault, or is not what you ordered, send us a photo. We cover the return cost and you can choose a replacement or a full refund including the delivery fee. Faults can be reported up to 6 months after delivery.
