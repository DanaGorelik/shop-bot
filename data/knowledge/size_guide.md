# Size guide

## How to measure

Measure over light clothing with a soft tape held snug but not tight. Chest: around the fullest part. Waist: around the narrowest part of your torso. Hips: around the fullest part, about 20 cm below the waist.

## Tops, knitwear, dresses and outerwear

Body measurements in centimetres:

| Size | Chest | Waist | Hips |
|------|-------|-------|------|
| XS | 80-85 | 62-67 | 86-91 |
| S | 86-91 | 68-73 | 92-97 |
| M | 92-97 | 74-79 | 98-103 |
| L | 98-104 | 80-86 | 104-110 |
| XL | 105-112 | 87-94 | 111-118 |

## Jeans and shorts sized by waist

These are sized by waist in inches. Body waist measurement in centimetres:

| Waist size | Body waist |
|------------|------------|
| 28 | 70-73 |
| 30 | 74-78 |
| 32 | 79-83 |
| 34 | 84-88 |
| 36 | 89-94 |

All jeans have a 32-inch (81 cm) inside leg. We hem jeans for free in the shop.

## Between two sizes

If your measurements fall between two sizes, choose the larger size for knitwear and outerwear, and the smaller size for linen pieces, which are cut loose and soften with wear. Each product also has its own fit note, for example whether it runs large or is cut slim.

## Converting from other sizing systems

Approximate equivalents: XS is EU 34 / UK 6, S is EU 36 / UK 8, M is EU 38-40 / UK 10-12, L is EU 42 / UK 14, XL is EU 44-46 / UK 16-18.
